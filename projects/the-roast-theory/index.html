From Farm to Cup

The Story of the Bean

01

Origin

From carefully selected farms in high-altitude regions of Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. We visit every farm. We know every farmer.

02

Harvest

Only the ripest cherries, hand-picked at peak season. Each cherry is sorted, washed, and sun-dried with patience.

03

Roasting

We roast in small batches, relying on sight, sound, and smell rather than automation. Every roast profile is hand-tuned.

04

Packaging

Sealed within hours of roasting. Our bags are designed for freshness and beauty — because details matter.

05

Your Cup

Extracted perfectly to bring out the subtle notes of each unique origin. Every sip tells a story.
