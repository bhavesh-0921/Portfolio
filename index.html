Premium Brand Website · Concept Project

Atelier Aurea

RoleDesign & Development
EmotionCalm · Timeless
PaletteIvory · Charcoal · Bronze
TypeConcept Project

Overview

Atelier Aurea is a fictional luxury interior design studio specializing in bespoke interiors for high-end residences, boutique hotels, and private penthouses. Average project budgets: $80K–$2M. The website needed to establish trust with affluent decision-makers — not sell, but signal quality.

Challenge

Luxury brands online fall into two traps: excessive flash or bland templates. Neither builds trust with high-net-worth individuals. The goal was quiet confidence — premium without pretension. A website that feels like walking into a beautifully designed Scandinavian home.

Audience

Primary: High-net-worth individuals aged 35–65 — architects, entrepreneurs, CEOs, investors, hotel owners. They're not browsing. They're evaluating. They already have money. They need confidence. Secondary: Luxury developers, hospitality brands, real estate firms.

Research

Studied premium architecture studios: Norm Architects, Olson Kundig, Studio McGee. Analyzed editorial design from Aesop and Apple. The pattern: typography carries the design. Photography proves the work. Whitespace signals premium. Copy stays short and confident.

Strategy

Editorial, photography-led experience with restrained typography and generous whitespace. Single-page flow optimized for consultation bookings, not e-commerce. Every section answers one question: "Can I trust these people with my space?"

Design System

Warm whites, charcoal, and muted bronze. Typography: Instrument Serif for headings (timeless), Inter for body (clarity). Every color, spacing, and component decision traced back to one word: calm.

Responsive Design

Mobile feels editorial, not squeezed desktop. Typography scales fluidly. Navigation becomes an elegant drawer. Photography stacks vertically. Spacing is preserved — whitespace isn't sacrificed for mobile.

Outcome

Designed to increase perceived brand value and encourage consultation inquiries through a more focused user journey. Because this is a concept project, no conversion metrics are claimed.

Reflection

Luxury is restraint. The hardest part wasn't adding — it was removing. Every element that survived earned its place through purpose. Nothing decorative without reason.

Product Design · SaaS Operations Platform

PulseFlow Operations Platform

RoleLead Product Designer & Dev
EmotionControl · Efficiency
PaletteNear Black · White · Electric Blue
TypeConcept Project

Overview

PulseFlow unifies company operations, project backlogs, approvals, and performance reporting into a single intelligent workspace. Designed to eliminate spreadsheets and tool switching.

Challenge

Growing businesses lose velocity by spreading workflows across dozens of separate tabs. The design challenge was creating a complex operational dashboard that feels fast, reduces user anxiety, and is highly accessible.

Audience

Operations managers, COOs, founders, and project leads at mid-sized businesses (50-500 employees). They evaluate systems based on efficiency, control, and reliability.

Research

Analyzed B2B tools like Linear, Stripe Dashboard, and Raycast. Identified the value of keyboard-first navigation, layout grids, progressive disclosure of settings, and high contrast styling.

Strategy

Create a unified dark-mode B2B workspace simulator. Support a multi-view router (analytics, settings, board, flow builder) with minimal click counts. Every UI card must prove functional utility.

Design System

Solid backgrounds, clean 1px borders, electric blue accents. Geist and Inter fonts. Radius set at 16px. Forms feature clear error fields, loading skeletons, and empty state guides.

Outcome

Designed as a production-quality dashboard layout ecosystem demonstrating reusable widgets, interactive pipeline builder, and responsive canvas sizing.

Reflection

Enterprise product design is not about decoration. Every component must earn its place. A clean design doesn't hide complexity; it clarifies it.

E-commerce · Brand Identity · Web

The Roast Theory

RoleBrand & Web Design
EmotionWarmth · Comfort
PaletteEspresso · Sand · Olive
TypeConcept Project

Overview

The Roast Theory is a fictional specialty coffee brand created to explore how branding, storytelling, e-commerce, and editorial design can come together into one cohesive digital experience.

Challenge

Most coffee websites focus strictly on products but fail to communicate craftsmanship or create an emotional connection. We needed to design a brand that feels alive, where buying coffee feels like a ritual rather than a transaction.

Audience

Coffee enthusiasts aged 24–45 — remote workers, creatives, founders — who appreciate craftsmanship, slow mornings, and intentional living.

Research

Studied premium coffee brands like Blue Bottle, editorial magazines like Kinfolk, and boutique retail experiences. Observed how photography, typography, and whitespace influence perception of quality.

Strategy

Use immersive imagery, restrained typography, and narrative-driven sections to make the user feel connected to the product before asking them to buy. The website feels like walking into a quiet café on a rainy morning.

Design System

Warm, natural tones. Espresso browns, sand, cream, and muted olive greens. Typography pairs the elegant Cormorant Garamond with the clean General Sans to ensure readability while maintaining an editorial feel.

Outcome

Designed as a concept project demonstrating brand storytelling, premium e-commerce UX, and cohesive visual identity.

Reflection

Branding is storytelling compressed into symbols. The best brand identity doesn't describe what you do — it shows how you make people feel.

UX Design · Frontend · Storytelling

Atlas Horizon

RoleUI/UX & Frontend
EmotionWonder · Curiosity
PaletteDeep Forest · Ivory · Gold
TypeConcept Project

Overview

Atlas Horizon is a fictional luxury expedition company designed to explore immersive storytelling, destination-first UX, and premium travel branding.

Challenge

Most travel websites emphasize prices and booking mechanics instead of inspiring users emotionally. The challenge was to sell travel through emotion and storytelling, rather than discounts and flights.

Audience

High-net-worth individuals, executives, and adventure enthusiasts aged 30–65 who want an unforgettable life experience, not just another vacation.

Research

Analyzed luxury hospitality brands (Aman), travel magazines (National Geographic, Patagonia Stories), and premium resort websites. Focused on how they communicate exclusivity, trust, and aspiration.

Strategy

Use photography, narrative copy, and restrained interactions to encourage users to imagine themselves on the journey before asking them to inquire. Slower scrolling, larger imagery, and a cinematic feel.

Outcome

Designed as a concept project exploring premium travel storytelling, immersive UX, and high-end digital branding.

Reflection

The best UX doesn't feel like UX. When someone forgets they're using an interface and just feels the experience — that's when the design works.

Agency Website · UI/UX · Motion

Nova Studio

RoleEngineering & Branding
EmotionConfident · Visionary
PaletteBlack · White · Electric Blue
TypeConcept Project

Overview

Nova Studio is a fictional premium creative agency created to demonstrate advanced frontend engineering, motion design, branding, and agency-level UX. An Awwwards-style site focused on extreme minimalism and massive typography.

Challenge

Most agency websites prioritize visual effects over communication, resulting in impressive first impressions but poor clarity. The challenge was building an identity that feels visionary and bold, while remaining incredibly clear and accessible.

Audience

Luxury brands, tech startups, and enterprise companies evaluating digital partners. They look for precision, rhythm, and proof of craftsmanship.

Research

Studied the philosophy behind Cuberto, Locomotive, Basic/Dept, and Apple. Focused on structure, pacing, and information hierarchy rather than just copying aesthetics. Evaluated how motion builds trust.

Strategy

Create an editorial-first experience where typography, layout, and motion guide the user through the agency's philosophy, process, and selected work. Avoid decorative fluff — "The typography IS the design."

Outcome

Designed as a concept project exploring agency branding, advanced interaction design, and scalable frontend architecture.

Reflection

A great digital experience shouldn't just be viewed; it should be felt. Purposeful motion and precise typography elevate a website from a brochure into a digital product.

Digital Growth Strategy Case Study

Project Spasht_Banda Case Study

RoleGrowth & Strategy Lead
EmotionIntimacy · Depth
PlatformInstagram (Organic)
Reach1.1M+ Views / 30 Days

Overview

Project Spasht_Banda documents the creative strategy and publishing systems built to scale a poetry-focused writing brand to over 1.1 million organic views in a 30-day period without paid advertising.

Challenge

Saturated feeds make audience retention difficult. Simply posting writing does not work; it requires content design systems, hook layouts, pacing, and optimization framework metrics.

Audience

18-35 year olds who value authentic creative writing. They bookmark content that mirrors their feelings and share messages that help them express themselves to their friends.

Research

Analyzed top poetry and creative accounts. Discovered that simple black-text-on-warm-background designs dramatically outperform colorful graphics because they mirror paper print, evoking wabi-sabi wamth.

Strategy

Content Pillars

Organized around Micro Poetry (hooks), Relatable Thoughts (shares), and Healing (saves) to drive distribution.

Pacing Strategy

Used pattern interrupts, sentence length changes, and timed text lines to keep users reading past the 3-second mark.

Creative Framework

Every asset follows: Hook → Setup Pause → Emotional Build → Peak Line → Reflection Loop.

Systems Workflow

Structured production: Idea → Copywrite → visual layout → publish → review metrics → iterate.

Results

1.1M+Organic Views
0Paid Ads Spend
100%Original Content

Why This Matters

Proves I understand copywriting, content systems, growth marketing, and audience psychology by delivering real-world reach results rather than concept designs alone.

Reflection

A growth system isn't luck; it's engineering emotional resonance. People share what represents their identity. If you design for identity, reach takes care of itself.
